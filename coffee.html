Coffee for Education

Exploring how coffee growers, cafés, businesses, churches, organisations and individuals can connect their work and generosity with children's education.

Every Child, Every Chance

We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, grow and build a brighter future. Through All God's Children Foundation, we work to support children and families in our community, including through education support and essential school supplies.

We welcome conversations with coffee growers, roasters, cafés, businesses, churches and individuals who share this vision. Together, we can explore ideas that connect coffee with opportunities for children to access education.

Connecting Coffee with Children's Futures

Coffee brings people, communities and businesses together. We would like to explore whether these connections can also help create educational opportunities for children who need support.

We are open to practical ideas, conversations and partnerships that reflect the interests and capacity of everyone involved.

Why Education Matters

A child's education can open doors to knowledge, confidence and future opportunities. Yet some children face barriers such as school fees, uniforms, scholastic materials and other essential needs.

All God's Children Foundation works with children and families in the Kamengo community in Mpigi District, Uganda. Our education work aims to help children access learning opportunities and encourage them to keep working towards their futures.

Our Shared Vision

We want to build connections between people who care about coffee, community and education, and explore practical ways to help children learn and thrive.

Each potential collaboration can be discussed individually so that expectations, responsibilities and the intended use of any support are clear.

Start with a Conversation

We are at the stage of exploring ideas and welcoming conversations. No particular coffee product or partnership arrangement is assumed.

1. Share Your Idea

Tell us about your work, your interests and the kind of connection you have in mind.

2. Explore What Is Practical

We can discuss possible approaches, resources, responsibilities and whether the idea fits our education mission.

3. Agree on Next Steps

If there is a suitable opportunity, we can discuss the details and agree on clear next steps together.

Built on Trust and a Shared Purpose

We value honest communication, responsible collaboration and the wellbeing of the children and families we serve. Any proposed initiative will need to be discussed clearly, including its purpose, commitments and how it could contribute to educational opportunities.

All God's Children Foundation's registration is currently in progress in Uganda. We aim to communicate openly about our status and the arrangements for any potential collaboration.
