What We Do

At All God's Children Foundation (AGCF), we work with communities to help children access education, learn essential life skills, and grow in safer and more supportive environments.

Our programmes focus on education, school supplies, water safety, access to safe water, family and community support, and mentorship. Explore each programme below to learn more about our work.

Education Support

We support children facing barriers to education by helping with school fees, uniforms, scholastic materials, and essential school requirements. Our aim is to help children continue learning and work towards a brighter future.

Explore Education Support

School Supplies Drive

We mobilise school supplies and learning materials for children who need them. These essentials help learners prepare for school and participate more confidently in their education.

Explore School Supplies

Water Safety Campaign

We promote water safety awareness and drowning prevention among children and communities. Through educational sessions and practical demonstrations, we aim to help children recognise water hazards and understand safer behaviour around water.

Explore Water Safety

Safe Water Access

We support community efforts to improve access to safer water. Our borehole project documents the process from manual digging and drilling to pump installation, top construction, and community handover.

Explore Safe Water Access

Family & Community Support

We engage with families and communities to understand children's needs and provide practical support where possible. Our work encourages community participation and stronger support systems for children.

Explore Community Support

Mentorship & Life Skills

We encourage children through mentorship, educational guidance, and life-skills activities. These opportunities help children build confidence, develop positive habits, and think about their future goals.

Explore Mentorship

Community Borehole Construction and Safe Water Access

Access to safe water is important for children's health, family wellbeing, and community development. AGCF's borehole project page shares photographs documenting the work at different stages, including manual digging, drilling, pump installation, borehole top construction, and community handover.

Visit the project page to view the photographs and learn more about the community water-access initiative.

View the Borehole Project

Working Together for Children

Our programmes are guided by a commitment to children's wellbeing, dignity, and protection. We value collaboration with families, volunteers, community members, and partners who share our goal of helping children learn and thrive.

All God's Children Foundation's registration is currently in progress in Uganda. We continue to develop our programmes and strengthen our community-focused approach.
