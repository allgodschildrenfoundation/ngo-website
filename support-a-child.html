Education Changes Lives

Help a Child Stay in School

At All God's Children Foundation (AGCF), we believe that a child's family circumstances should not determine their opportunity to receive an education. Some children face poverty, the loss of parents, and difficult caregiving situations that make access to education especially challenging.

Through our education support work, we seek to help children access schooling and essential learning needs. With the support of caring individuals and partners, we hope to reach more children who need an opportunity to learn.

Meet the Children

These stories reflect the circumstances of children whose education AGCF is working to support. We share them to help people understand why educational support matters.

Education Support

Mugerwa

Primary 1

Mugerwa is a bright young boy from a family experiencing significant financial hardship. When AGCF first encountered him, he had never attended school.

Recognising his potential and the importance of education, AGCF took him on for educational support. He is now receiving support to attend school and begin building the foundations for his future.

His story reminds us that a child's ability and potential can exist even when their family lacks the resources to provide access to education.

How you can help: Support the ongoing educational needs of children like Mugerwa, including school fees, scholastic materials, uniforms and other essential learning requirements.

Nampijja

Primary 3

Nampijja is a young girl who has lost both parents and is being cared for by her grandmother, who lives with a disability. Their circumstances make meeting everyday needs and educational costs particularly difficult.

Despite these challenges, Nampijja deserves the opportunity to continue learning, develop her abilities and look forward to a future with more possibilities.

Support for her education can help ease the financial burden on her caregiver and help her continue pursuing her schooling.

How you can help: Contribute towards school fees, uniforms, scholastic materials and other agreed educational needs.

A Brother and Sister — Twins

Primary 3

These twins, a boy and a girl, have lost both parents and are being cared for by their aunt. The family is experiencing serious financial hardship, making it difficult to meet the children's educational needs.

Both children deserve the chance to continue attending school, learn alongside their peers and develop their individual abilities.

Helping the twins with their education can make a meaningful difference to their learning journey and provide practical support to the relative caring for them.

Practical Support

What Can Your Support Provide?

Contributions can help meet verified educational needs, depending on the child's circumstances and the resources available.

School Fees

Help children access and continue their education by contributing towards agreed school costs.

Uniforms and School Essentials

Help provide school uniforms, shoes, bags and other essential items children need to participate in school.

Scholastic Materials

Support access to exercise books, pens, pencils and other appropriate learning materials.

Continued Educational Support

Help AGCF respond to children's changing educational needs through follow-up and appropriate support planning.

Responsible and Transparent Support

AGCF aims to direct educational support towards identified needs and to communicate responsibly with supporters about the use of contributions. The type and level of support depend on available resources and the child's circumstances.

Supporters are welcome to contact us to discuss current needs, appropriate ways to contribute and how support can be coordinated. We do not publish children's private records or personal family details on this page.
