Creating Opportunities Through Education

All God's Children Foundation (AGCF) works with children and families in Kamengo, Mpigi District, Uganda, to help address barriers that can prevent children from accessing education.

Through educational assistance, family engagement and community support, we aim to help children continue learning with dignity, encouragement and hope.

How AGCF Supports Education

Depending on assessed needs and available resources, our education support may include:

  • School-fee assistance for selected children.
  • Uniforms and essential school items.
  • Exercise books, pens, pencils and other scholastic materials.
  • Educational encouragement, mentorship and follow-up.
  • Engagement with parents and caregivers to understand children's needs.

Our Commitment

We believe every child deserves a chance to learn. AGCF seeks to work responsibly with families and communities, using available resources to help children access educational opportunities.
