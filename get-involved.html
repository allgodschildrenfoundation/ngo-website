Get Involved

Partnership can start with a conversation, a shared skill, a school connection or a practical act of support. Together, we can help create better opportunities for children and families in our communities.

Choose How You Would Like to Help

We welcome thoughtful collaboration that respects children, families and the community. Explore the different ways you can contribute to the work of All God's Children Foundation.

Individuals and Families

Share our work, organise a small fundraiser with friends, offer relevant skills or contact us about supporting education and child-safety activities.

Churches

Churches in Uganda can invite AGCF to discuss a short, child-friendly water-safety awareness session during a Sunday School service. We also welcome introductions to other congregations.
