Partnerships That Put Children First

All God's Children Foundation (AGCF) welcomes conversations with organisations, churches, schools, businesses, community groups and individuals who share our commitment to children's wellbeing and community development.

Based in Kamengo Sub-county, Mpigi District, Uganda, our work focuses on education support, water safety awareness, safe water access, family and community support, and mentorship.

Churches and Faith Communities

Work with us on child-focused community outreach, educational encouragement, water safety awareness and connections with communities that may benefit from our programmes.

Schools and Educators

Explore appropriate collaboration on children's learning, school supplies, educational encouragement, mentorship and water safety awareness.

Nonprofit Organisations

Discuss shared goals, community knowledge, referrals, technical support and opportunities to coordinate complementary activities.

Businesses and Corporate Partners

Explore responsible ways to contribute skills, materials, professional services or support for clearly defined community activities.

International Partners

We welcome enquiries from churches, foundations, community groups and organisations outside Uganda interested in learning about AGCF's work and exploring appropriate collaboration.

Community and Technical Experts

Share knowledge and experience in education, child safeguarding, water safety, public health, agriculture, communications, monitoring or organisational development.

Areas for Collaboration

What Could We Work on Together?

Partnership discussions can begin with a shared interest and develop around community needs, available resources and agreed objectives.

Education and School Support

Help improve children's access to learning materials, educational encouragement, mentorship and suitable school support activities.

Water Safety and Child Protection

Explore awareness sessions, learning resources and community activities that help children recognise water hazards and learn safer behaviour.

Family and Community Wellbeing

Discuss practical initiatives that respond to identified needs among children and families and strengthen community participation.

Skills, Knowledge and Resources

Consider training, technical guidance, learning materials, volunteering or other contributions that fit an agreed activity.

Our Approach to Partnership

We value respectful relationships, transparency, accountability and collaboration that responds to real community needs. We aim to agree on the purpose of each activity, the roles of those involved and how progress will be reviewed.

Children's safety, dignity and privacy remain central to our work. Any joint activities involving children should follow appropriate safeguarding practices.

AGCF's registration is currently in progress in Uganda. Potential partners are welcome to contact us to discuss our current work, organisational status and suitable opportunities for collaboration.
