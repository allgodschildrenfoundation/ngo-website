School Supplies Drive

Helping children access the essential learning materials they need to participate in school, build confidence and pursue a brighter future.

Supporting Children Through Education

All God's Children Foundation works with children and families in Kamengo, Mpigi District, Uganda, to help address barriers to education. Through our School Supplies Drive, we mobilise essential scholastic materials that help children prepare for school and take part in classroom learning.

By working with community members and supporters, we aim to make learning materials more accessible to children who need them.

What the Drive Supports

Depending on available resources and children's needs, the drive may support access to exercise books, pens, pencils and other essential scholastic materials.

Our Community Approach

We believe that supporting education is a shared responsibility. We work to connect community needs with the generosity of people who want children to have better opportunities to learn.

Our Goal

Our goal is to help children begin and continue their learning with essential materials, while encouraging families and communities to value and support every child's education.

Help a Child Be Ready to Learn

Your support can help provide essential school materials and give children more opportunities to participate in education.
