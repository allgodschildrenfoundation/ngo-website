Water Safety Campaign

Water Safety Saves Lives — Be Safe, Be Aware. Promoting water safety awareness and drowning prevention among school children.

Protecting Children Through Water Safety Awareness

All God's Children Foundation is developing a water safety campaign to help children recognise water-related dangers and learn practical ways to stay safe around ponds, rivers, lakes, wells, dams and other water environments.

Our pilot campaign focuses on five primary schools in Kamengo, Mpigi District, Uganda, with the long-term aim of expanding water safety awareness to more communities.

What the Campaign Teaches

  • Recognising dangerous situations around water.
  • Keeping a safe distance from unfamiliar or unsafe water areas.
  • Never entering the water to rescue someone without appropriate training.
  • Calling for adult help and alerting emergency responders when someone is in danger.
  • Understanding basic water safety rules at home, school and in the community.

How We Plan to Reach Children

The pilot is designed around school awareness sessions, practical demonstrations, educational games and role-play, posters and safety messages, and engagement with teachers and volunteers.

Where resources allow, the campaign will also support schools with suitable safety materials and equipment to reinforce the lessons.

Our Pilot Location

The initial campaign is planned for five primary schools in Kamengo, Mpigi District, Uganda. Lessons learned from the pilot will help guide future improvements and possible expansion.

Why Water Safety Matters

Children may encounter water hazards while travelling, playing or helping with everyday activities. Age-appropriate water safety education can help them recognise danger, make safer decisions and know when to seek help.

Help Us Promote Water Safety

We welcome collaboration with schools, churches, community groups and supporters who share our commitment to protecting children and promoting water safety awareness.
