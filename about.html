About All God's Children Foundation

Every Child, Every Chance.

We work with children, families and communities to promote education, safety and opportunities for a better future.

Who We Are

All God's Children Foundation (AGCF) is a community-focused foundation working to help children access opportunities to learn, grow and live safer, healthier lives. We believe every child deserves care, encouragement and the chance to build a better future.

Our Mission

To support vulnerable and underprivileged children through education, safety awareness, family support and community initiatives that promote wellbeing and opportunity.

Our Vision

A future where every child has the opportunity to learn, feel safe, develop their potential and participate in building a stronger community.

Our Values

The principles that guide our work with children, families and communities.

Every Child Matters

We believe each child deserves dignity, care, protection and opportunities to learn and develop.

Compassion and Service

We approach our work with kindness, respect and a commitment to serving children and families facing challenges.

Community Partnership

We work alongside families, volunteers, schools, community leaders and partners to support lasting positive change.

Safety and Protection

We promote children's safety, wellbeing and protection in our activities and community engagement.

Accountability

We value responsible use of resources, honest communication and transparency with the people and partners who support our work.

Hope and Opportunity

We encourage children to believe in their potential and pursue opportunities that can strengthen their futures.

How We Work

We believe meaningful change begins by listening to communities and responding to children's needs. Our work includes education support, school supplies, water-safety awareness, safe-water initiatives, family and community support, and mentorship and life-skills activities. We aim to work collaboratively, use available resources responsibly and learn from the communities we serve.

Rooted in the Community

AGCF's work is based in Kamengo Sub-county, Mpigi District, Central Region, Uganda. We work to support children and families while building relationships with community members and potential partners who share our commitment to children's wellbeing.

Be Part of a Child's Brighter Future

Whether you volunteer, partner with us, share our work or contribute to our programmes, you can help create more opportunities for children.
