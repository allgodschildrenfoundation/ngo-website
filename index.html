Who We Are

Working Together for Children and Communities

All God's Children Foundation (AGCF) is a community-focused foundation established in 2024. Our work is rooted in the belief that children deserve opportunities to learn, develop and live safely.

Based in Kamengo Sub-county, Mpigi District, Uganda, we work with families, volunteers, schools and community members to respond to local needs and support children's wellbeing.

  • Education and school supplies support
  • Water-safety awareness and drowning prevention
  • Safe water access initiatives
  • Family support, mentorship and community engagement

The foundation's registration is currently in progress in Uganda.

Protecting Young Lives

Water Safety Saves Lives

Water can present serious risks to children when safety knowledge and supervision are limited. AGCF is preparing a water-safety awareness initiative focused on helping school children recognise hazards and make safer decisions around water.

The planned pilot is focused on five primary schools in Kamengo, Mpigi District, with activities designed to make water-safety education practical and understandable.
