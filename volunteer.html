Volunteer With Us

Share your time, skills and experience to help create opportunities for children and strengthen communities.

Make a Difference

Make a Difference Through Service

All God's Children Foundation believes that meaningful community change grows when people work together. We welcome enquiries from individuals interested in contributing to our child-focused and community-based work in Kamengo, Mpigi District, Uganda.

Education Support

Help with suitable learning activities, educational encouragement and initiatives that support children's participation in school.

Water Safety Awareness

Support appropriate awareness activities that teach children about water hazards and safer behaviour around water.

Mentorship and Life Skills

Contribute relevant skills and experience to activities that encourage children's confidence, learning and development.

Community Engagement

Help with community activities, family engagement and practical initiatives that respond to local needs.

Professional and Technical Skills

Share relevant skills in communications, administration, digital technology, fundraising, project planning or other areas that may benefit the foundation.

Partnership and Outreach

Help build connections with schools, churches, community groups and organisations interested in supporting children.

Join Our Effort

Who Can Volunteer?

We welcome enquiries from people committed to respectful service, responsible conduct and children's wellbeing.

  • Community members who want to support local initiatives.
  • Teachers, educators and people with relevant learning skills.
  • People with water-safety, first-aid or child-development experience.
  • Professionals willing to share useful technical or organisational skills.
  • Churches, schools and groups interested in appropriate community service.
  • International supporters interested in exploring suitable collaboration.

Children's Safety Comes First

AGCF is committed to protecting children's safety, dignity and wellbeing. Volunteers must respect our safeguarding requirements, follow activity guidance and maintain appropriate boundaries when interacting with children.

Volunteer participation is subject to discussion, suitability checks where appropriate, and any relevant safeguarding requirements for the activity. The safety and best interests of children come first.
