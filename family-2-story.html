A Grandmother's Strength: Supporting a Family and a Child's Education

A story of caregiving, perseverance and the hope of building a more stable future for a grandmother and the grandchildren who depend on her.

At All God's Children Foundation (AGCF), we meet families whose daily lives involve challenges that are not always visible to the wider community. This is the story of a grandmother living with a disability who continues to care for her grandchildren despite the difficulties she faces.

A grandmother carrying a great responsibility

The grandmother has difficulty walking and is the main caregiver for her grandchildren. Their parents are not present in their lives, leaving her with the responsibility of caring for them and meeting their daily needs.

Despite her physical difficulties, she continues to look for ways to provide for the family. Whenever she can, she goes to people's gardens to earn money through digging and fetches water for others in exchange for a small income.

The work is physically demanding, especially given her disability. Yet she continues doing what she can to provide food and other basic necessities for the children in her care.

Supporting a child's education

When AGCF learned about the family's circumstances, we listened to their situation and considered how we could help. One child from the family was selected to receive educational support through the foundation.

This support includes assistance with school fees and essential school supplies, helping the child continue learning. We believe every child deserves an opportunity to receive an education, regardless of the difficulties their family may be experiencing.

Where resources allow, AGCF also provides basic assistance to help ease the family's immediate burden. We recognise that supporting a child's education is connected to the wider circumstances in which that child lives and learns.

Her determination deserves an opportunity

Her experience reflects a difficult reality for some caregivers: they must provide for children while managing physical limitations and very limited opportunities to earn a reliable income.

We want to help her explore a more sustainable way to support her household. Rather than depending only on strenuous physical work that is difficult for her to manage, she could have an opportunity to earn income through a small shop.

A modest shop selling everyday essentials could offer a more manageable source of income and help her meet the family's needs with greater dignity. This remains a goal that requires support and resources to make it possible.

This story is shared to explain the family's circumstances and the support being sought. The children's privacy and dignity should be protected when sharing photographs or personal details.

Help Her Build a More Stable Future

AGCF is seeking support to help this grandmother establish a small shop, while continuing to support the selected child's education and provide basic family assistance when resources permit.

If you would like to support this effort, please contact our foundation to discuss how you can contribute or partner with us. Every contribution should be managed transparently and directed toward the agreed purpose.
