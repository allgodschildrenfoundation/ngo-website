Josh's Story: A Dream of Becoming a Doctor

A meeting that reminded us why every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow and pursue a dream.

Education Support  |  All God's Children Foundation

A Meeting I Will Not Forget

I don't usually share stories like this, but this meeting felt important.

Recently, I met Josh, a 10-year-old boy who, at the time we met, had never had the opportunity to attend school.

One Wednesday morning, while other children were in class, I found him at a dumping site collecting plastic bottles. Not by choice, but because this was what life had required him to do to survive.

One Question, One Powerful Dream

I asked Josh why he wasn't in school. His answer stayed with me: he had never had the opportunity.

Then I asked him what he wanted to become when he grew up. His answer came without hesitation.

"Doctor."

That one word carried so much hope. Despite the circumstances he was facing, Josh had a dream for his future.

The Reality Behind His Dream

Josh told me that he collected plastic bottles every day to help him survive. He earned about UGX 150 per kilogram. That small amount represented the difficult circumstances surrounding his access to education.

We spent time talking about his life, his family and his hopes. We also shared a simple lunch. It was a small moment, but it gave me an opportunity to listen to him and learn more about his situation.

Why AGCF Exists

As I left, one thought stayed with me: this should not be the limit of a child's possibilities.

Meeting Josh reminded me why I started building All God's Children Foundation. AGCF seeks to support children who want to learn but face circumstances that make education difficult to access.

I understand what it means to have a dream and not have the support needed to pursue it. Every child deserves an opportunity to discover their abilities and work toward a brighter future.

Every Child Deserves a Chance

Josh's story is a reminder that behind every child is a person with hopes, abilities and dreams. A child's circumstances should not be the only thing that defines what is possible for their future.

Through its education support work, AGCF hopes to help children and families facing barriers to learning. Meaningful progress requires practical support, community involvement and people who believe every child deserves an opportunity.

If Josh's story speaks to you, you can help by learning more about AGCF's work, sharing our mission or contacting us to explore ways to support children's education.
