Make a Difference

Help Create Opportunities for Children

Many families face challenges in meeting children's educational and other essential needs. AGCF works with local communities to identify needs and develop practical responses that support children to learn, stay safe and grow.

We welcome people, churches, businesses, community groups and organisations who would like to contribute their resources, skills, time or networks.

Our Commitment

Responsible and Transparent Support

We recognise that every contribution represents trust. AGCF aims to communicate clearly about programme needs, use contributions responsibly and share appropriate updates about activities and progress.

Before making a contribution, please contact us to confirm the current need, the intended use of the support and the appropriate method for contributing.

The foundation's registration is currently in progress in Uganda. We will provide relevant organisational and contribution details directly when responding to enquiries.

Before You Contribute

  • Contact AGCF to discuss the intended support.
  • Confirm the current programme need and arrangements.
  • Agree on how the contribution will be used.
  • Ask how updates about the supported activity will be shared.
